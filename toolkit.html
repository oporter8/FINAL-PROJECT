Room 2 / Engineering bay

Repair the control panel

The panel shows three useful commands. One clue tells you how to read the long transmission in the final room.

  • A=1, B=2, ... Z=26
  • THAN-ZIE is a code word, not a number.
  • Hyphens inside a word are not separators.

Use the 26-letter alphabet: A is 1 and Z is 26. The first entry in that alphabet is also the second hint.
